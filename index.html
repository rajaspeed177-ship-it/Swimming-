An Architectural Oasis Dedicated to Aquatic Excellence

AquaZone Pools combines high-performance sports training with five-star wellness relaxation. Designed for competitive swimmers, fitness enthusiasts, and families alike, our sanctuary delivers year-round heated waters, eco-friendly ozone purification, and world-class hospitality.

Facilities & Amenities

Designed for Champions, Families & Seekers of Calm

Four specialized aquatic zones engineered for peak athletic performance, playful family recreation, and deep restorative tranquility.

Olympic-Sized Lap Pool

Heated to 28°C year-round

10 competition-standard 50-meter lanes fitted with anti-wave dividers, digital pacing systems, and precision touchpads for lap workouts.

  • ✓ 50m × 25m FINA specifications
  • ✓ Dedicated fast, medium & slow lanes
  • ✓ Underwater acoustic pace system

Splash & Play Park

Safe, fun zones for kids

Interactive shallow splash pad featuring gentle water arches, soft mini-slides, tipping water buckets, and zero-depth entry areas.

  • ✓ Zero-depth slip-resistant rubber deck
  • ✓ Shaded parent lounge loungers
  • ✓ Full-time certified splash monitors

Spa & Sauna Lounge

Jacuzzi & relaxation beds

Rejuvenate sore muscles in our 38°C hydrotherapy whirlpool, Scandinavian cedar wood sauna, and herbal eucalyptus steam suite.

  • ✓ Multi-jet hydro-massage Jacuzzi
  • ✓ Finnish dry sauna & cold plunge
  • ✓ Heated ergonomic stone loungers

Swim Academy

1-on-1 private coaching

From infant water safety to adult stroke biomechanics and competitive pacing, our certified coaches tailor sessions to your individual goals.

  • ✓ Video stroke analysis technology
  • ✓ Beginner, intermediate & master tiers
  • ✓ Small 1:4 maximum group class ratio
